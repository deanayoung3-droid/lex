Your website, answering back.

Lex is a small text box in the corner of your site. Visitors ask what they'd ask a salesperson. Lex answers from your own pages, shows them the part that proves it, and hands you the ones who want to talk.

What it does

Everything a good salesperson does on your website, without the pop-ups.

  • Answers from your site

    Lex reads your website every day, plus your docs or help center, and answers only from those and the notes you write. When it doesn't know, it says so and offers a call.

  • Shows, not just tells

    Asked about pricing, it takes the visitor to your pricing page, with a five-second countdown they can stop. Asked about the page they're on, it scrolls to that section.

  • Suggests the right question

    When a visitor's pointer rests on a section, a question about it appears above the text box. One click asks it.

  • Moves people forward

    Buttons for booking a call, a demo or signing up, with your links and your words. When someone leaves their email, you get it in Slack or any webhook.

  • Tells you what people want

    Trends groups every question into topics and shows what Lex couldn't answer. Chats has every conversation in full.

Install it

About ten minutes, on any website that can add a script tag.

  1. Run Lex

    You need Node.js 24 and an OpenAI API key. The database is built in. For a real site, use Docker with automatic HTTPS.

    git clone https://github.com/deanayoung3-droid/lex.git
cd lex
npm install
npm run setup   # secrets, OpenAI key, admin password
npm run dev     # http://localhost:3000

  2. Tell it about your business

    Enter your website's address, and what a good salesperson would know: pricing, who it's for, how you compare. Add your booking, demo and sign-up links.

  3. Paste one line

    Before the closing </body> tag, on Framer, Webflow, WordPress, Squarespace, Shopify or your own site. Then turn Lex on.

    <script src="https://lex.yourcompany.com/lex.js" defer></script>
